Privacy Policy — PipeCore Lite

Effective date: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

PipeCore Lite ("the App") is a piping engineering reference tool that provides dimensional data for pipes, flanges, fittings, and line blanks based on ASME standards. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Waitlist Registration (Optional)

If you choose to join the PipeCore Pro waitlist through the app or website, we collect:

  • Name — to personalize our communication
  • Country — to understand our user base geographically
  • Email address — to notify you when PipeCore Pro launches

Through the contact form, we may also collect a free-text message you choose to send us (questions, suggestions, or feedback), used solely to respond to your inquiry.

This information is submitted voluntarily. It is stored securely through Netlify Forms and used only to contact you about the PipeCore Pro launch or to respond to your message. This data may be collected through the app or website. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing dexterasupp@gmail.com.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

Beyond the optional waitlist above, PipeCore Lite does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Camera, microphone, or media access
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Financial or payment information

2.3 Advertising and Conversion Measurement

The website (lite.usepipecore.app) uses Google Ads conversion tracking to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. This service may collect and use:

  • Cookies set by Google on your browser
  • Device identifiers and advertising IDs
  • Aggregated conversion events (e.g., app installs, page visits)

This data is processed by Google LLC under their own privacy policy. No personally identifiable information is shared with Google Ads beyond what is described above. You can opt out of Google's use of cookies for advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

2.4 Local Storage

The App stores user preferences locally on your device using the browser's localStorage mechanism. This includes settings such as preferred unit system (US/SI), display language, and last selected module. This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to any server, and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

3. Internet Connection

The App requires an internet connection to load engineering data from our servers at lite.usepipecore.app. These requests contain only the technical parameters needed to retrieve the requested data (e.g., pipe size, pressure class) and do not include any personal information. Standard web server logs may record IP addresses as part of normal server operation; these are not used to identify individual users.

4. Third-Party Services

The App and website use the following third-party services:

  • Netlify — hosts the website, API functions, and processes waitlist form submissions (Netlify Forms). Netlify may collect standard technical information such as IP addresses and request timestamps as part of their infrastructure operation.
  • Google Ads — used for conversion tracking as described in section 2.2. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy

PipeCore Lite is intended for professional use by engineers and technical personnel. The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
